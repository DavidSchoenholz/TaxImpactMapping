          About this data

          How the numbers are made: the effective rate is a bill divided by its assessed value. The area-specific part is the effective rate minus the city-wide rate from the levy by-law. The ratio effect re-spreads each year's city-wide levy using 2020 tax ratios. Scripts: scripts/build_story.py and scripts/analysis/.
